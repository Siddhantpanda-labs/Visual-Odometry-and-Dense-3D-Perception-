Problem Statement
Why Does Classical Visual Odometry Fail?
02 / 08

1. Scale Ambiguity

Monocular 5-point epipolar geometry recovers translation $\mathbf{t}$ only up to an arbitrary scale factor $\lambda$. Accumulating scale errors over consecutive frames causes severe metric trajectory drift.

2. Motion Degradation

Sudden turns, potholes, and mechanical vibration produce motion blur. Spatial gradient loss breaks optical flow tracking, causing catastrophic RANSAC outlier explosion.

3. Epipolar Degeneracy

When baseline translation approaches zero ($\mathbf{t} \approx 0$) or features lie on planar asphalt, the Essential Matrix $\mathbf{E} = [\mathbf{t}]_\times \mathbf{R}$ becomes ill-conditioned, hallucinating wild translations.

System Architecture
Gated Multi-Sensor Perception Pipeline
03 / 08

Three Measured Robustness Gates

  • Laplacian Blur Rejection: Evaluates $\text{Var}(\nabla^2 I)$. Frames with variance below 100 are rejected before matching.
  • Parallax Keyframe Gating: If mean optical flow is $< 1.5\,\text{px}$, vehicle is stationary; holds pose to prevent random-walk drift.
  • Homography Degeneracy Guard: If $N_H / N_E > 0.80$, pure rotation branch is activated, preventing scale explosion.

Dual-Sensor Odometry Engine

  • Monocular Mode: KLT corner tracking, 5-point RANSAC Essential matrix, cheirality depth test.
  • Stereo Mode ($b=0.54\,\text{m}$): Sub-pixel stereo triangulation ($Z = f \cdot b / d$), EPnP 3D-to-2D pose solver.
  • Sliding-Window BA: Optimizes last $W=5$ keyframe poses and landmarks with Huber loss Levenberg-Marquardt.

Validation & Failure Diagnosis
Real-World Highway Validation: Diagnosing & Fixing Unexpected Turns
05 / 08

Root Cause of Sudden Turns

  • Low-Inlier SVD Degeneracy: At frame 67, inliers dropped to 0 due to shadows/passing cars. recoverPose returned an inverted 179.8° rotation matrix.
  • Orientation Corruption: Unchecked rotation multiplied into $\mathbf{R}_w$, flipping the world reference frame 90° sideways.
  • Sign Ambiguity: Epipolar geometry can flip the sign of $\mathbf{t}$, reversing forward motion.

Engineered Solution & Result

  • Minimum Inlier Gate: Rejects poses with $< 15$ inliers, holding pose state and replenishing corners.
  • Kinematic Angular Guard: Rejects relative rotation $> 6.0^\circ$ ($> 150^\circ$/s is physically impossible in 40ms).
  • Forward Motion Prior: Enforces $t_z > 0$ along camera optical axis.
  • Result: Smooth, continuous 1,118.0 m trajectory along the true highway curvature!

Viva Defense
Key Questions & Defensible Answers
07 / 08

Q1: Why not end-to-end Deep Learning VO?

Answer: Deep models (DROID-SLAM) require powerful GPUs, have non-deterministic inference latencies, and fail silently out-of-distribution. Classical geometric VO guarantees deterministic bounds and transparent failure diagnostics.

Q2: Why did Stereo drop drift by 8.6×?

Answer: Monocular VO cannot observe absolute scale from epipolar rays. Stereo triangulation provides an immutable metric baseline ($b=0.54\,\text{m}$), anchoring 3D point depth without scale drift.

Q3: Why Huber loss in Bundle Adjustment?

Answer: Standard $L_2$ squared loss quadratically amplifies outliers. Huber loss transitions linearly past $\delta = 1.5\,\text{px}$, preventing tracking outliers from distorting camera pose estimates.

Q4: Why does standalone Stereo beat Stereo+BA?

Answer: On CPU, iterative Levenberg-Marquardt with large landmark matrices slows throughput to 8.5 FPS. In production, BA must run asynchronously on a background worker thread.
